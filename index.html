About us

A trusted last-mile distribution brand headquartered in Butere.

Registered in 2013, Jixels Technologies is a trusted brand with its headquarters in Butere, Kakamega County, in the heart of Western Kenya.

We specialize in retail and last-mile distribution of mobile phones across Kenya and beyond borders, offering both outright purchase and flexible pay-go options to make smartphones more accessible and affordable to all.

Over the years, we have expanded our services to include green energy solutions, consumer electronics like TVs and other life-changing products.

At Jixels Technologies, we are committed to bridging the digital and energy gaps in underserved communities by delivering reliable products right to their doorstep.

Vision and mission

Driven by urgency, powered by trust.

To become Africa's most dynamic and reliable last-mile distributor by connecting every household with essential digital devices and other life changing products, one device at a time.

FAQ

Questions customers ask about phones on installment in Kenya.

Can I buy a smartphone on pay-go?

Yes. Availability depends on the model, partner terms and your location. Message the team to confirm deposit, daily or monthly payment options.

Which phone brands are available?

Jixels works with smartphone options including Samsung, OPPO, vivo, ZTE, HONOR and M-KOPA models, subject to current stock.

Do you offer phone repair in Butere?

Jixels provides phone diagnostics, repair guidance and device support through trained technicians and service teams.

Where can I find a Jixels outlet or agent?

Jixels serves areas including Butere, Kakamega, Kisumu, Busia, Bungoma, Luanda, Mumias, Sabatia and Mombasa. WhatsApp the team for the nearest support point.

What should I ask before choosing an installment phone?

Ask for deposit, daily or monthly payment, total term, warranty, ID requirements, stock status and after-sales support.

About us

A trusted last-mile distribution brand headquartered in Butere.

Jixels was registered in 2013 and incorporated as a limited company in 2021. Headquartered in Butere, Kakamega County, Jixels serves communities across Kenya through retail, last-mile mobile phone distribution, flexible pay-go access and reliable customer support.

The company has grown beyond phones into green energy solutions, consumer electronics such as TVs, and other life-changing products that support homes, work, learning and opportunity.

Vision and mission

Driven by urgency, powered by trust.

Vision

To become Africa's most dynamic and reliable last-mile distributor.

Jixels aims to connect every household with essential digital devices and other life-changing products, one device at a time.

Mission

To deliver technology to every corner through a people-powered network.

The mission is to rapidly deliver digital devices and technological solutions through a bold, agile distribution network that makes technology accessible, affordable and impactful.

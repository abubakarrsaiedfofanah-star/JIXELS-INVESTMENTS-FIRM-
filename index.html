About us

A trusted last-mile distribution brand headquartered in Butere.

Jixels was registered in 2013 and incorporated as a limited company in 2021. Headquartered in Butere, Kakamega County, Jixels serves communities across Kenya through retail, last-mile mobile phone distribution, flexible pay-go access and reliable customer support.

The company has grown beyond phones into green energy solutions, consumer electronics such as TVs, and other life-changing products that support homes, work, learning and opportunity.

Registered 2013 Incorporated 2021 Butere HQ

Vision and mission

Driven by urgency, powered by trust.

Vision

To become Africa's most dynamic and reliable last-mile distributor.

Mission

To deliver technology to every corner through a people-powered network.

The mission is to rapidly deliver digital devices and technological solutions through a bold, agile distribution network that makes technology accessible, affordable and impactful.

How it works

Partnerships, procurement, training, selling and aftercare.

Jixels connects brand supply to real households through a practical last-mile system. Products move through trained teams, community agents and outlet support so customers get devices, setup help and service after purchase.

01 Partnerships

Global and local brand partnerships supply trusted products.

02 Procurement

Bulk procurement and inventory management keep stock moving.

03 Training

05 Aftercare

Customer aftercare and feedback complete the loop.

The 3A problem

Jixels solves the barriers that keep customers disconnected.

Affordability

Availability

Remote areas often lack reliable access to phones, electronics and trusted product support.

After-sales service

Customers need help after purchase, from complaints and repairs to setup and feedback.
